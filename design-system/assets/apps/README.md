The lockups and app icons of the three Queer League apps (Manager, Planner, Referee), supplied on 06.11.2026. Never recolour, outline, stretch or redraw them. Originals live in `../../../assets/apps/`.

Per app (`manager`, `planner`, `referee`):

- `qvl-<app>-logo-light.png` is the lockup for light grounds (petrol wordmark, grey "Queer League").
- `qvl-<app>-logo-dark.png` is the lockup for dark grounds and Nacht (white wordmark). Unlike the league logos it needs no white plate.
- `qvl-<app>-app-icon.png` (1024 px) and `qvl-<app>-app-icon.svg` are the square app icon on the petrol chamfer tile, for favicons, home-screen icons and About dialogs.

Use the lockup where an app introduces itself (login, About dialog, mails), the app icon where space is square. The league logos in `../logo/` stay the mark of the Queer Volleyball League itself.
