`QVL_CI_COLORPALLET.pdf` is the supplied CI sheet: the colour roles (Hauptfarbe Petrol, Akzent 1 Orange, Akzent 2 Gelb, Akzent 3 Weiß, Grundfarbe Grau, Hintergrund Hellgrau), their tints and the typefaces (Bebas Neue for headlines, Oswald Light and Regular for body). Its values are transcribed into `tokens.json`; open the PDF to check a value against the source, not to build from.
