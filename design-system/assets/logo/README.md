The four supplied Queer League lockups as PNG with a transparent ground. Use them through `Logo`; never recolour, outline, stretch or redraw them.

- `qvl-logo-long-petrol-orange.png` is the default long lockup on light grounds (`Logo variant="long-petrol"`).
- `qvl-logo-long-orange-petrol.png` is the long lockup with an orange "Queer" (`variant="long-orange"`).
- `qvl-logo-short-petrol-orange.png` is the stacked short lockup (`variant="short"`).
- `qvl-icon-petrol-orange.png` is the QVL block icon (`variant="icon"`).

In dark mode a logo always sits on a white chamfer plate (`qvl-white`), because the files are raster and carry their own petrol ink. No SVG logo was supplied: small sizes and print will suffer.
